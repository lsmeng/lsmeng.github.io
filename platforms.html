Platforms

Web tools built on our research. They are free for academic use, exploratory and not peer reviewed unless a paper is cited on the tool page. Each states what its numbers mean and which reference they are measured against.

SeisForge

Uncertainty and quality control for seismological parameters: upload a catalogue or model outputs and get calibrated scores, sets and uncertainties back.

SeisForge · Focal mechanisms

Calibrated first-motion polarity probabilities, focal-mechanism sets with a stated coverage level, and the effect of mechanism uncertainty on stress inversion. Upload a CSV, download the calibrator.
Live · exploratory · seisforge.imageolab.com/focal

SeisForge · Machine-learning detection

Scores for earthquakes detected by machine-learning pickers: the calibrated chance that a seismologist would confirm a candidate event.
In preparation · Meng, Huang, Ma & Ma (2026), Can human seismologists verify machine-learning detected earthquakes?, EarthArXiv, doi:10.31223/X5MZ2D

Overview and terms: seisforge.imageolab.com.

Tsunami

Community tsunami fast-model service

Paint a fault slip on a map, get the offshore maximum water level from a neural surrogate trained on a shallow-water solver, and compare with the solver on request. Registration with an academic address.
tsunami.imageolab.com · trial service
