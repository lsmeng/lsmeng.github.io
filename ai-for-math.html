AI for Math

I use modern AI systems as collaborators in mathematical research: to explore conjectures, search for constructions, organize long case analyses, write verification software, and stress-test proofs. My current projects are in combinatorics, especially permutation patterns and graph labelling. Each item below links to the strongest public record available — arXiv for preprints, and GitHub or Zenodo for manuscripts, code, data, and certificates.

arXivPermutation patterns · 2026

Residual structure and growing inversion-monotonicity regions for 1324-avoiding permutations

Studies the inversion distribution of 1324-avoiding permutations. The revised manuscript proves monotonicity through k ≤ 2n+6, obtains fixed-defect structure and growing regions, and records the remaining unrestricted conjecture as open.

The arXiv record may briefly display the v1 title while the submitted revision is processed.

arXivGraph labelling · 2026

Trees of odd order with at most two vertices of degree two are edge-graceful

Proves a new case of Lee's edge-graceful tree conjecture using zero-sum block partitions and Langford sequences, with symbolic checks and an independently re-checked census of 2,245,070 trees.
