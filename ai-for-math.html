My current projects are in combinatorics, especially permutation patterns and graph labelling. PreprintNumber theory · 2026

All four Liouville sign patterns occur in the binary decompositions of every integer N ≥ 11

Proves that every integer N ≥ 2 outside {2,3,4,5,6,9,10} is a sum a+b with (λ(a), λ(b)) equal to any prescribed pair of signs, removing the Generalised Riemann Hypothesis and friability conditions from Mangerel's theorem for this existence statement. The key step is a one-exclusion rigidity argument at primes; the main results are formalized in Lean 4 with only the standard axioms.

arXivPermutation patterns · 2026

Residual structure and growing inversion-monotonicity regions for 1324-avoiding permutations

Studies the inversion distribution of 1324-avoiding permutations. The revised manuscript proves monotonicity through k ≤ 2n+6 (computer-assisted), obtains fixed-defect structure and growing regions, and records the remaining unrestricted conjecture as open.

arXivGraph labelling · 2026

Trees of odd order with at most two vertices of degree two are edge-graceful

Proves a new case of Lee's edge-graceful tree conjecture using zero-sum block partitions and Langford sequences, with symbolic checks and an independently re-checked census of 2,245,070 trees.

arXiv ↗ Supplement ↗

Extended by the manuscript in preparation on edge-graceful trees with few vertices of degree two (below).

PreprintCombinatorial trees · 2026

Nonexistence of order-18 Leech trees and of Leech spiders of order at least five

Proves that no Leech tree of order 18 exists and that no spider of order at least five is a Leech tree, supported by exact searches, clean-room replication, and independently checkable certificates.

PreprintAI evaluation · 2026

How far can a commercial decision model's probabilities be trusted? A calibration audit of Jev against open and general-purpose classifiers

Audits the probabilities returned by TypeSafe's Jev 1.13.0 on SST-2 and AG News: how their calibration depends on the task and the question form, how the 0.01 quantisation limits routing, what temperature scaling and conformal prediction can repair, and how the comparison with open zero-shot classifiers depends on the benchmarker's choices of checkpoint and label descriptions.
