Privacy Policy

This page covers a small personal tool (Google Cloud project "DungeonHolder") that Lingsen Meng uses to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to his own YouTube channels and to back up his own files to Google Drive.

The tool is used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account, through the permissions he grants at sign-in. It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person. Access tokens are kept only on the owner's computer, and access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
